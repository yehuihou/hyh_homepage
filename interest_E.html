My interest

Detective Fiction
I'm really into classic detective fiction, mystery films and shows,
especially those with a Japanese style.
I love stories that involve locked-room mysteries, architectural tricks, or eerie family secrets.
I'm also a fan of TV series like Agatha Christie's Poirot and Agatha Christie's Marple, as well as manga and anime like The Kindaichi Case Files and Detective School Q.
Action Figures
I'm really into collecting 6-inch action figures of my favorite anime characters — mostly from series like Dragon Ball, Ultraman and other tokusatsu shows (mainly the S.H.Figuarts line by Bandai),
as well as EVA and JoJo's Bizarre Adventure.
Trading Card Game
I highly recommend the Digimon Trading Card Game ( DTCG)!
It features excellent game mechanics, great interactivity and gameplay feel, and beautifully illustrated card art.
Cycling
Although I'm new to cycling, it really feels amazing — it's such a great way to relax and clear my mind.
